description: 教授として現在の研究方針を批評する（意義・新規性・弱点・次の一手）
---

Launch the `professor` subagent to assess the current research direction.

$ARGUMENTS

If arguments are given, pass them as the specific question to focus on. Otherwise ask
for a general assessment of the direction.

Relay the assessment to the user in full — this is the output they asked for, not
something to summarise away. Add your own view only if you disagree with it, and say
plainly that you are disagreeing and why.
