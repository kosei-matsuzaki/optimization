---
description: フラットな視点で研究の現状を整理する（主張せず、測定条件つきで記述する）
---

Launch the `librarian` subagent for a neutral account of what the research has
established.

$ARGUMENTS

If arguments name a scope (a theme, a document, a date range), pass it through.

Relay the account. Do not add recommendations on top of it — the point of this
command is that the user gets the state without anyone's framing, including yours.
If the librarian reports contradictions or stale records, offer to fix them as a
separate step rather than folding the fix into the summary.
