Competition Setup[1]:
• 16 test problems (PID = 1, 2, ..., . . . , 16)
• 4 dimensionalities ( = 2, 5, 10, 20)
• 15 problem instances (PIN = 1, 2, . . . , 15) 
• one run for each 
The total number of optimization runs = 16 × 4 × 15 = 960.
Computer code in Python and MATLAB
No ad-hoc parameter tuning
